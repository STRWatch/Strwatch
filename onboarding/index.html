Getting started

Welcome to STRWatch.

Complete these steps to start receiving real-time regulation alerts for your properties. Takes about 5 minutes.

Setup progress 0 / 5 complete
1
Choose your markets
Select the cities where you have properties — you'll get alerts when regulations change.
Required ~1 min
▾

Head to the Markets tab and select every city where you operate. STRWatch tracks regulation changes, enforcement actions, and permit deadlines for each market you add.

  • We currently monitor 30 US markets with active scraping — Nashville, Austin, Denver, NYC, LA, and more
  • Each market shows its data source (Legistar API, SODA API, ArcGIS, page monitoring)
  • Pro plan = unlimited markets, Free = 1 market
Go to Markets →
💡 Add every market where you have properties or are considering investing. You can always remove markets later — better to have too many alerts than too few.
2
Set up alert preferences
Choose how and when you want to be notified.
Required ~1 min
▾

Configure your notification channels and urgency thresholds. High-urgency alerts (permit deadlines under 30 days, enforcement actions) can go to SMS. Routine updates go to email.

  • Email alerts — all plans, daily or weekly digest
  • SMS alerts — Pro and Agency, for urgent deadlines only
  • Urgency filters — choose what qualifies as "urgent" for your portfolio
3
Review your compliance snapshot
See every regulation, deadline, and requirement for your properties.
Required ~2 min
▾

Once your markets are selected, STRWatch generates a compliance snapshot — a complete picture of what's required to operate legally in each market.

  • Active permits & licenses — what you need and where to get them
  • Tax obligations — city, county, and state tax rates and filing deadlines
  • Upcoming deadlines — renewal windows, registration dates, compliance milestones
  • Recent changes — ordinances passed in the last 90 days that affect your properties
📋 Pro tip: Share the PDF snapshot with your property manager or CPA. They'll need the same deadline information you do.
4
Run a market scan or explore the map
Research any US market or browse all 30 monitored cities.
Recommended ~1 min
▾

Two ways to research markets:

  • Market Scans — enter any US city and generate a pre-investment regulation report. Permit requirements, tax rates, enforcement risk, and key deadlines.
  • Regulation Map — interactive map of all 30 monitored markets, color-coded by severity. Click any city for detailed stats and rules.
  • Scans for our 30 tracked markets are enriched with live data from our scraping infrastructure
Run a scan → Open regulation map
5
Invite your team (optional)
Add co-hosts, property managers, or partners.
Optional ~1 min
▾

If you work with a property manager, co-host, or business partner, invite them to your STRWatch account. Everyone stays on the same page about deadlines and regulation changes.

  • Shared compliance dashboard — everyone sees the same deadlines
  • Individual alert preferences — each person controls their own notifications
  • Agency tier supports unlimited team members
